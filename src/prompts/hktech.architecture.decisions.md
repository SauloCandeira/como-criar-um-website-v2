# HKTech – Architecture Decisions (ADR)

## ADR-001: Firebase as Auth Provider
Status: FINAL
Reason: Speed, security, ecosystem fit
Trade-off: Vendor lock-in

## ADR-002: MyAlien as Immutable Identity
Status: FINAL
Reason: Stable identity layer
Trade-off: Migration complexity

## ADR-003: Event-Driven Learning
Status: FINAL
Reason: Traceability and LGPD compliance
Trade-off: More complex state management

## ADR-004: MyBot Non-Autonomy
Status: FINAL
Reason: Ethical, legal and UX safety
Trade-off: Reduced automation

## ADR-005: Monolith First, Modular Later
Status: FINAL
Reason: Avoid premature microservices
Trade-off: Initial coupling
